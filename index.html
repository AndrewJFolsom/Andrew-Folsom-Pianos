The Teacher’s Perspective

I combine an active background as a piano instructor with rigorous technical training. I understand that a piano is more than furniture; it is a complex machine that must be stable, responsive, and clear for a musician to thrive. Whether it is a routine maintenance tuning or a complex mechanical regulation, I treat every piano with professional rigor and a commitment to long-term instrument health.

My Services

Pitch Tuning

Expert fine-tuning to ensure your piano is stable at standard A440 concert pitch, no matter the season. Standard sessions are $140 (ask about local student studio discounts).

Regulation

Meticulous adjustments to internal mechanical components—including let-off and aftertouch—for a lighter, more responsive touch and precise control. Custom quotes available based on scope.

Maintenance & Repair

Fast solutions for sticky keys, uncooperative pedals, and basic structural issues. Minor repairs and climate-control advice can often be bundled directly with a tuning session for maximum value.
